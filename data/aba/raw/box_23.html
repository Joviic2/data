Preview
Ivan Rudež, trener Cibone:

„Čeka nas novo kolo ABA lige. Dolazi nam ekipa koja je nova u ligi i u regiji, iz Bratislave. To je dobar izazov za nas i prilika da izjednačimo bilancu na 1-1 u otvaranju ovogodišnje ABA lige. Pozivam sve da dođu, napune dvoranu i budu nam vjetar u leđa.“

Vigo Bart, igrač Cibone:

„U ponedjeljak nas čeka teška utakmica u ABA ligi. Iako smo u Ligu prvaka ušli odlično, što svi znaju, u ABA ligi smo prvu utakmicu kiksali i nadamo se da ćemo to ispraviti. Igramo kod kuće, pred našim navijačima. Ovaj put bih pozvao sve, znam da je ponedjeljak, ali dođite, podržite nas i napravimo dobru atmosferu.“

Dejan Jakara, Slovan head coach: 

"Playing against Cibona? Sounds good, but it also sounds hard. We will try to break them down and, of course, I think we have had enough time to prepare. We had five or six days since our last game, and we tried to use them the best we could. "

Róbert Rožánek, Slovan player:

"I'm curious to see what we can do in Zagreb, and I truly believe we'll grab our first win there. We definitely have to step up our defense compared to the Igokea game. We completely fell apart defensively in the first half back then, and even though we fought hard, it made everything so much tougher. I believe we've learned from that and will prove it with a much better performance against Cibona."
