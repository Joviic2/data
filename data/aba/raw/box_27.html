Preview

Mihai Silvășan, U-Banca Transilvania Head Coach:

"This is our first game this season against a EuroLeague team, a very good and highly competitive team with a deep roster. It is a source of pride and an honor for us to be able to play against a team of BC Dubai’s caliber. However, we certainly want to compete and give ourselves a chance to win the game. That is our goal and what we all want. We want to continue building our team spirit and improving our game."

Uroš Plavšić, U-Banca Transilvania Player:

"We all know that BC Dubai is among the strongest teams in the EuroLeague when it comes to the talent and experience of its players. We need to focus on what we have to do and give our very best. Preparation is very important for us, and we need to approach this game in the best possible way. With the help of our fans, I hope we can get a positive result."

Xavi Pascual, Dubai Basketball head coach:

"Tomorrow’s game is very important, especially coming off a defeat. The next game is always the most important one, and we have to be ready to compete from the first minute. Cluj are a very good team with great coach, players and excellent shooters who can hurt you from anywhere on the court. They come into this game after a very convincing win in Europe, so we know we’ll have to perform at a high level."

Mamadi Diakite, Dubai Basketball player:

"We have to learn from the mistakes we made in our last game and be mentally ready from the very beginning. We’re facing a great opponent in an atmosphere that will make it a great place to play basketball."

Comments
Mihai Silvășan, U-BT Cluj-Napoca Head Coach:

"Mixed feelings after this game. I really liked our first half, but as I told the players in the locker room, games aren’t won after 20 minutes. We need to be consistent and play well for all 40 minutes, especially against a team like Dubai, with so much talent. They have a great coach and outstanding players. To have a chance to win a game like this, we need to be close to perfect in terms of focus and intensity. Unfortunately, our third quarter was really poor. They outscored us 32-14, and I think the game was decided during that stretch. We struggled to find good shots, missed layups and turned the ball over too easily. We didn’t show the same determination, and I think we were afraid of the possibility of winning the game. This needs to be a very important learning experience for us. It is what it is. Congratulations to Dubai. We need to move forward and focus on our next game, which is very important for us in the EuroCup."

Xavier Pasqual, Dubai Basketball Head Coach:

"First of all, I want to congratulate the team from Cluj and their coach on an outstanding game. They have been playing very well for several years, and they are having another fantastic season, so I wish them the best of luck going forward. I also want to congratulate the club on the impressive attendance. We had another great atmosphere here today. For us, it was a game in which we had to fight hard. I think we played a little better in the first half than the score suggested because we missed a lot of open shots, as well as several shots around the basket. But that’s basketball: you have to make those shots, and we didn’t. In the second half, we were more solid offensively and scored 56 points. We moved the ball very well. Even though our three-point shooting was terrible today, making only five of 22 attempts, we finished the game with 92 points and secured a very important win for us."
