Preview
Dragan Bajić, Vienna Head Coach: 

“I put this team together, and I have no doubt these guys will step on the floor, give everything they have and compete for every possession. That’s the standard I expect from them. Right now, all of our focus is on this game. The key is staying locked in and executing what we want to do on both ends for 40 minutes. If we bring the right fight, focus and effort, we’ll give ourselves a chance to get a positive result.”

Nikola Jovanović, Vienna player:

"We’re focused and ready to get on the court. Opening the season in front of our home fans means a lot to us. Cluj is an experienced, high-quality team, so we need to bring the right energy from the opening minute and execute our game plan on both ends of the floor.”

Mihai Silvășan, U-BT Cluj-Napoca Head Coach:

"We’re looking forward to the game in Vienna because we’re very eager to get back on the court after the tough loss in Rome. It’s not easy to deal with a defeat like that, but it’s our job to do so. We have to move forward. We’ve been preparing and will continue to prepare for Vienna, our first opponent in the ABA League. We want to get the win and continue building our game as a team."

Bogdan Nicolescu, U-BT Cluj-Napoca player:

"First of all, I’m happy that we’re finally getting started in this competition and that all the organizational issues have been resolved. It’s a difficult moment for us after a tough loss in the BKT EuroCup. That’s exactly why I’m glad we have the opportunity to put that performance in Rome behind us and play another game right away, where we can show a different attitude. I think it’s an opportunity we all have to take advantage of. For me, this will be my debut in the ABA League and, from what my teammates who have already played in the competition and the coaching staff have told me, every opponent can be challenging. So I expect a difficult game in Vienna. But, as I said, this is a great opportunity for us to start finding our form, rebuild our confidence and continue developing our game."

Comments
Dragan Bajić, BC Vienna Head Coach

First of all, congratulations to Cluj on the win, and I also want to thank our fans for coming out in good numbers and supporting us throughout the game. There were things I liked tonight. At times, especially defensively, our concentration, energy and effort were at a very high level. But there were also stretches where we made mistakes, and a team with Cluj’s quality is going to punish you for that. It was a tough, physical game. We had a poor second quarter, but I liked the character we showed after that. We fought our way back several times, cut the deficit and gave ourselves a chance to compete for the game. Down the stretch, one or two possessions can make the difference. That’s basketball. I don’t want to make excuses. We are a young team and we still need experience, especially in games like this and at this level. Playing in the ABA League and in Europe will help us grow. This team is going to compete. That is part of my character and that is the identity I want this team to have. Every night, against every opponent, we have to bring maximum effort. Tonight we showed that we can fight our way back into a game. There are still a lot of things we need to improve on both ends of the floor, but the effort, character and commitment have to stay at this level."

Mihai Silvășan, U-BT Cluj-Napoca Head Coach:

"I’m very happy that we got our first official win of the season. It was important for us, especially after a poor performance in Rome. Coming here and responding the way we did was not easy. Our frontcourt was important tonight, but this was a team win. We respected Vienna, stayed disciplined possession by possession and found good solutions offensively. Scoring 95 points and finishing with 27 assists are very good numbers for us. In the fourth quarter, the key was creating quality shots. Sometimes you make them, sometimes you miss them, but what matters to me is the quality of the possession. We made some big threes, but they came from good execution and the right reads, not from random one-on-one plays. We want to keep improving and continue building a team where everybody understands his role and knows exactly what he has to do. We want to develop our identity step by step throughout the season. Vienna is a good team and this is not an easy place to play. They are in their second ABA League season and I wish them all the best. For us, the focus is always on the next game. We don’t want to look too far ahead. Now we have Dubai, a EuroLeague team, coming up, so another very difficult challenge is waiting for us."
